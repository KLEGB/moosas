Defining Weather and Wind

You define weather that you want CONTAM to use based on the type of simulation you want to perform. You will either define steady state weather or transient weather. It is best to perform a set of steady state simulations prior to performing transient simulations. This may enable you to better identify any problems that may arise from working with transient weather. ContamW also provides a means to verify that you have your wind related data input correctly (See Checking Wind Pressure Data).
