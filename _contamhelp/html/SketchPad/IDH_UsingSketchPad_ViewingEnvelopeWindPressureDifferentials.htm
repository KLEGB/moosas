Viewing Envelope Wind Pressure Differentials

ContamW provides a feature that allows you to visualize the effects of wind upon a building envelope. A graphical display of wind pressures at each of the flow paths that are adjacent to the ambient zone can be viewed on the SketchPad (see Checking Wind Pressure Data in the Defining Steady State Weather and Wind section). This feature enables you to verify input of wind speed and direction data with respect to the orientation of the building envelope to the wind.
