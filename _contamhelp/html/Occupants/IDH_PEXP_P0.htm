Occupant – Occupancy Schedule Properties

Occupant Number: This is the number that appears in the status bar when you highlight an icon. ContamW automatically assigns this number to each exposure icon once it is defined. This number could change as you add and remove occupants to or from a project. Each time you save a project, the occupant numbers are reassigned. Numbers are assigned beginning on the top level, starting from the upper-left corner of the SketchPad, moving left-to-right and top-to-bottom of the SketchPad.

Occupant Week Schedule: Use the Occupancy Week Schedule to define the movement of this occupant within the building (see Working with Schedules).
